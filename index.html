Commonly needed documents

A general guide. Exact requirements depend on the department and the service, so confirm with us before you start.

Passport

  • Aadhaar card
  • Proof of date of birth
  • Address proof
  • Passport-size photo

PAN card

  • Aadhaar card
  • Passport-size photo
  • Signature
  • Mobile linked to Aadhaar

Certificates

  • Identity and address proof
  • Supporting records (e.g. school or ration records)
  • Recent photo

Exam & job forms

  • Photo and signature (exact size)
  • Educational certificates
  • Category certificate if applicable
  • Email and mobile number

GST / Udyam

  • PAN and Aadhaar of owner
  • Business address proof
  • Bank account details
  • Photo of proprietor

Insurance

  • Vehicle RC
  • Previous policy, if any
  • Owner ID proof
FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are my files safe in the free tools?

Yes. The free tools run entirely in your browser, so your files are not uploaded to any server. They load their libraries from the internet, so you need to be online.

What happens to files I send for printing?

They go to the shop's print system. Printed jobs are automatically deleted after 3 days.

Can I print Word, Excel or PowerPoint files?

Please convert them to PDF first, or message us on WhatsApp and we will help.

Are you a government office?

No. We provide assistance with government portals and documentation. We are not a government-authorised issuer for every service, and availability depends on the relevant department.

How do I know what a service will cost?

Fees vary by service and department. Message us the service name on WhatsApp and we will share the charges up front. Print rates are shown on the print form.

How do I check my print job?

Use the "Check status" box in the Print Online section with the Job ID you received after uploading.
